About Aerolinas Drone Services

At Aerolinas Drone Services, we help businesses and individuals in Charlotte, NC showcase their work with a fresh perspective. Our services include drone photography, videography, construction progression, and branded content designed to elevate projects, strengthen marketing, and tell stories in a visually impactful way.

Founded by Nick Weston, Aerolinas Drone Services blends technical precision with creative storytelling. The goal is simple: create visuals that help people understand, market, document, and remember important spaces and moments.

Whether capturing construction sites, real estate, or branded campaigns, we do not just provide drone footage. We create visuals that inspire, connect, and make an impact.
